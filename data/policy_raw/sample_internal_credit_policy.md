# Sample Internal Credit Policy for MSME Unsecured Lending
*Classification: Sample Internal Policy - Non-confidential Demonstration*
*Policy Code: POL-MSME-2025-V1*

## 1. Overview and Purpose
This document outlines the credit risk assessment criteria for Micro, Small, and Medium Enterprises (MSMEs) applying for unsecured working capital and short-term term loans up to INR 50,00,000 (Fifty Lakhs).

## 2. Eligibility Criteria
- **Clause ELIG-01 (Entity Vintage):** The applicant enterprise must have a minimum operational history of 24 consecutive months evidenced by GST registration and bank statement continuity.
- **Clause ELIG-02 (Minimum Turnover):** The enterprise must demonstrate an annualized turnover of not less than INR 25,00,000 verified through aggregate GSTR-3B filings or bank deposits over the preceding 12 months.
- **Clause ELIG-03 (Eligible Entity Types):** Sole proprietorships, partnerships, LLPs, and Private Limited Companies registered in India.

## 3. Financial Metrics & Underwriting Thresholds
- **Clause FIN-01 (Debt Service Coverage Ratio - DSCR Proxy):** The estimated DSCR proxy must be greater than or equal to 1.25x. If DSCR proxy is between 1.10x and 1.24x, loan terms require mandatory committee referral.
- **Clause FIN-02 (Average Monthly Balance - AMB):** The average monthly balance across the applicant's primary operating bank account over the past 6 months must equal or exceed 10% of the requested monthly EMI.
- **Clause FIN-03 (Bounced Cheque / Inward Return Threshold):** An applicant must have zero inward return instances due to financial failure (insufficient funds) over the most recent 3 months, and not more than 2 instances in the preceding 12 months.
- **Clause FIN-04 (EMI Burden Ratio):** Total proposed debt obligations (existing verified EMIs plus proposed loan EMI) shall not exceed 50% of the net operational monthly surplus.
- **Clause FIN-05 (Counterparty Concentration):** Inflow concentration from any single customer/buyer must not exceed 40% of the total 12-month banked revenues.

## 4. Document Verification & Completeness
- **Clause DOC-01 (Verification Requirement):** All critical document fields (Account numbers, GSTIN, Inflow/Outflow aggregates, Invoice totals) must possess an extraction confidence score of >= 0.85. Any field below this threshold must be human-reviewed before memo generation.
- **Clause DOC-02 (Inconsistency Threshold):** Discrepancies between turnover reported in GSTR filings and turnover deposited in bank statements exceeding 20% must trigger a referral.

## 5. Delegation of Authority & Approval
- **Clause AUTH-01 (Automated Recommendation):** Loans meeting all mandatory criteria with DSCR >= 1.50x and clean banking history may be recommended for APPROVAL.
- **Clause AUTH-02 (Referral Conditions):** Any condition triggering a referral rule requires a human credit officer sign-off with documented mitigating commentary.
- **Clause AUTH-03 (Decline Rule):** Bounced cheques exceeding 3 instances within 6 months, or DSCR below 1.0x, mandate an automatic DECLINE recommendation.
